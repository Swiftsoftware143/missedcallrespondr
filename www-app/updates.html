What’s new

Every change below is live in your console. Newest first.

20 November 2026

  • Real call events are processed. A Telnyx call event now updates the call, instead of being acknowledged and dropped.
  • Your workspace signature. Set the line printed under your logo in every message, right in the panel — the guide’s Email Branding section shows where.
  • Team invites never lose a password. If the password email does not leave, you are handed the password and the roster records that it never reached them.
  • Workspace list export. Take the workspace list out as a CSV file.
  • The inbox opens without a second sign-in. The mail-client door carries your session through.
  • One guide, both hosts. The user guide is the same documentation at both addresses again.

Earlier this month

  • Honest sentences. A suppressed receipt is no longer reported as sent.
  • Quieter bounces. Test addresses on the brand domain no longer bounce against the sending domain.
